Company Overview

About ASL

Our Company is engaged in the business of manufacturing of forged products and press shop for sheet metal products.

Our products have varied applications and are used in various industries like railway, defense, and automobile.

Our existing unit has a machine shop for the rough & finish machining of components, heat treatment facilities, in-house die making facilities, CNC & VMC horizontal and vertical centres, light, medium & heavy fabrication, assembly and sheet metal press shop.
